Main findings

  • Men earn ~22% more than women on average (₹80.8k vs ₹66.1k), with almost identical ratings — worth an HR pay-equity review.
  • Pay tracks performance only weakly (Spearman ρ = 0.25); several high earners are rated 2 or below, and several low earners are rated 4.5+.
  • Newer join cohorts are paid more but rated lower — average salary rises from ₹65k (2019) to ₹85k (2022) while rating falls from 4.1 to ~3.3.
  • 55% of employees with known status are inactive; HR (75%) and Sales (71%) have the highest inactivity, Finance the lowest (33%).
